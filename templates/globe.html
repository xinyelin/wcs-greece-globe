World Culture Salon

A country, studied slowly

World Culture Salon is a program of One Culture Foundation, a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit for cultural diplomacy. Each chapter turns to one civilization and asks what of it still reaches us directly: through art, scholarship and the senses.

A Country Chapter is not an event. It is a civilization studied slowly.

A gathering becomes research; research draws in artists and scholars; their work shapes an online gallery and, in time, a record the public can enter. This page is that record for the Greece chapter, in its first edition: San Francisco, 2026.

    Every chapter stays open

    Greece is the first chapter, and it does not close. Each new edition comes from a city where the culture lives and is shaped by a guest editor.
